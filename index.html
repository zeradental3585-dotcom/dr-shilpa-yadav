Why It Matters

A missing tooth doesn't stay a small problem

Left untreated, tooth loss changes more than your smile — it reshapes your bite, your jawbone, and eventually your face. An implant is the only option that stops this chain reaction at the root.

🦴

Bone loss

The jawbone under a missing tooth begins to shrink within months — the longer you wait, the more bone (and future treatment options) you lose.

↔️

Shifting bite

Neighbouring teeth drift into the gap, throwing off your bite alignment and putting uneven pressure on the teeth that are left.

🪞

Facial aging

Bone loss around the jaw is one of the most common — and most avoidable — causes of a sunken, aged appearance later in life.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the implant procedure painful?

The implant site is fully numbed with local anaesthesia, so the procedure itself is not painful. Mild soreness for 1–2 days afterward is normal and managed with standard pain relief — most patients describe it as far more comfortable than they expected.

How long does a dental implant take?

The implant placement itself typically takes 30–60 minutes per tooth. The full process — placement, healing (osseointegration), and final crown — usually spans 3–6 months, though healing time varies by individual.

Can diabetic patients get dental implants?

In most cases, yes — well-controlled diabetes is not a barrier to implant treatment. Dr. Shilpa will review your medical history and coordinate on timing to ensure safe healing.

What should I eat on Day 1 after an implant?

Stick to soft, cool-to-lukewarm foods — yoghurt, khichdi, soups, mashed vegetables. Avoid hot, spicy, hard or crunchy food and drinking through a straw for the first 24 hours.

Is an implant better than a bridge?

For most patients, yes — an implant is the only option that preserves jawbone and doesn't require filing down healthy neighbouring teeth. A bridge can still be the right call in specific clinical situations, which Dr. Shilpa will walk you through.
