The 4 Stages

From first scan to final crown

1

Consultation & 3D Scan

A detailed exam plus a digital scan maps your jawbone and gum health, so the plan is built around your actual anatomy, not guesswork. This is also when your itemised quote is put together — nothing is guessed at before this step.

2

Implant Placement

A titanium implant post is placed precisely into the jawbone under local anaesthesia — most patients describe this step as far less uncomfortable than they expected. The appointment itself typically takes 30–60 minutes per tooth.

3

Healing (Osseointegration)

Over roughly 3–6 months, the implant fuses naturally with your jawbone — the biological step that gives implants their 15–25+ year lifespan. Dr. Shilpa checks on progress with periodic reviews during this window.

4

Crown Fitting

A custom-matched crown is fitted on top — shaped and shaded to blend in with your own teeth, so it looks and functions like the real thing. This is the final appointment in the process.

Timelines are typical ranges — your own treatment plan is confirmed after Dr. Shilpa examines your scan, discussed openly at your consultation.

Common Questions

The process, answered honestly

How many clinic visits does the whole process take?

Typically a handful of visits spread across 3–6 months: the consultation and scan, the placement appointment, a healing period with periodic check-ins, and finally the crown fitting. The exact number depends on whether grafting is needed.

What if I need a bone graft first?

If your digital scan shows the bone at the site is too thin for a straightforward placement, Dr. Shilpa will discuss a bone graft first — this adds healing time before the implant itself is placed, but it's a well-established, routine step, not a sign of a complicated case.
