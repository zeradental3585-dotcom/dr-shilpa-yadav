Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the implant procedure painful?

The implant site is fully numbed with local anaesthesia, so the procedure itself is not painful. Mild soreness for 1–2 days afterward is normal — most patients describe it as far more comfortable than they expected.

How long does a dental implant take?

Placement itself typically takes 30–60 minutes per tooth. The full process — placement, healing, and final crown — usually spans 3–6 months.
