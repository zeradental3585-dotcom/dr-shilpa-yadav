Why It Matters

Why real reviews matter more for a dentist than almost anything else you buy

You can return a bad pair of shoes. You can't undo a filed-down tooth. That's exactly why patient reviews carry more weight for a dental decision than for most other purchases.

🔍

You can't "try before you buy"

Unlike most services, you commit to a dental procedure before you can judge the result — reviews are the closest thing to a trial run you get.

📊

Volume matters, not just rating

A 5★ average from 8 reviews tells you far less than a 4.9★ average from 467 — a large sample is harder to fake and harder to game.

🗣️

Specific details beat vague praise

"Great doctor!" could be written about anyone. A review that names the actual procedure — an implant, a root canal, a crown — is far more useful evidence.
